🌼 How to play

  • 🌱 Guess a five-letter word in six tries. Type with your keyboard or tap the letters below the garden.
  • 🌷 Green bloom — the letter is right and in the right spot. B
  • 🌼 Yellow bloom — the letter is in the word, but in the wrong spot. L
  • 🥀 Grey sprout — the letter is not in the word at all. X
  • 📅 Daily puzzle: everyone gets the same word each day. Come back tomorrow for a fresh one!
  • 🌱 Practice mode: endless words, no pressure — perfect for warming up.
  • 🏆 Win days in a row to grow your streak and plant a new flower in your garden. Share your garden with friends!
  • 📱 On your phone: tap the letters on the on-screen keyboard below the garden.
  • 💻 On a computer: type with your physical keyboard — letters to type · Enter to guess · Backspace to erase · P pause · R restart (P/R work on an empty row — while typing a word they are just letters).
