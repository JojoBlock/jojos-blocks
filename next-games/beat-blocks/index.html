How to play 🎵

1

Pick one of the three original tunes, then press Play. Blocks slide down four coloured lanes.

2

Tap a lane — or press D F J K — exactly when its block crosses the glowing beat line.

3

Perfect (within 60 ms) scores 300, Good (within 120 ms) scores 100. A late or missed block breaks your combo.

4

Combos raise your multiplier up to ×3. Finish the song for your grade: S, A, B or C.

5

Try Compose Mode: place blocks on the grid and hear your own tune loop. Bea dances when you’re on beat!
