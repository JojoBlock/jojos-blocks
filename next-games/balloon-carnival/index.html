📖 How to play

  • 🎯 Drag backwards from the slingshot to aim — the dotted line shows where your dart will fly. Release to fire!
  • 📱 Mobile: touch the slingshot, drag back, let go.
  • 🖥️ Desktop: click-drag with the mouse, or aim with ←→ + ↑↓ and fire with Space.
  • 🎈 You get 10 darts per stage. A pop is worth 10 points.
  • 🎪 Beat the stage's target score to clear it — the next stage is faster, windier, and star balloons drift sideways!
  • 🔥 Pop balloons in a row without missing to build a combo — up to +10 bonus points per pop!
  • ⭐ Balloons with a gold star hide a bonus — pop one for +25!
  • 🌈 Pop one balloon of each of the 6 colours to unlock the Rainbow Round: double points and +3 bonus darts.
  • 💨 Watch the wind — it pushes your dart sideways. Strong wind means aim into it!
  • ⌨️ Keyboard: ←→ aim · ↑↓ power · Space fire · P pause · R restart.
