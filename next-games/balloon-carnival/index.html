📖 How to play

  • 🎯 Drag backwards from the slingshot to aim — the dotted line shows where your dart will fly. Release to fire!
  • 🎈 You get 10 darts per round. A pop is worth 10 points.
  • ⭐ Balloons with a gold star hide a bonus — pop one for +25!
  • 🌈 Pop one balloon of each of the 6 colours to unlock the Rainbow Round: double points and +3 bonus darts.
  • 💨 Watch the wind — it pushes your dart sideways. Strong wind means aim into it!
