How to play

1

Tap a tile to cycle its colour — each tap hops to the next colour of the rainbow. Every colour also has its own shape, so you never have to rely on colour alone.

2

Fill the board so every row and every column holds each colour exactly once — no repeats anywhere.

3

Tiles that clash glow red and shake. Tap them again to fix the clash. Locked tiles 🔒 are givens — they never change.

4

Finish with zero mistakes for 3 stars. A new daily puzzle arrives every day — solve it daily to grow your 🔥 streak!
