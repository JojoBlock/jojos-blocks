Twin Trails

Two friends, two trails, one frosty dash! Steer the penguin and the seal pup — dodge the logs, leap the gaps, and catch the fish that match each scarf.

Best score: 0 · 🐧 coral fish for the penguin · 🦭 teal fish for Pip

How to play

Both friends run on their own trail. You control both at once!

👆Tap the left half of the screen to move the penguin — tap the left side to hop left, the right side to hop right. Tap the right half the same way for Pip.
🪵Dodge logs and gaps in the trail. Hitting one costs a heart. Each friend has 3 hearts — if either runs out, the run ends.
🐟Catch fish that match the scarf: striped coral fish for the penguin, spotted teal fish for Pip. A wrong-colour fish costs a heart — dodge it!
✨Catch a fish with both friends on the same beat for a SYNC bonus +25. Watch for paired fish — they arrive together!
💨Squeeze past a log in the next lane for a near-miss +2. The trails get faster the longer you run!
