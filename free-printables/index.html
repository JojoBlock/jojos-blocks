3

How do I download the free pack?⌄

Click the Download Free Taster Pack button on this page. The 6-page A4 PDF downloads directly to your device — no account, no sign-up and no email address needed.

How do I print the pages?⌄

Open the PDF on a computer or tablet and print on A4 paper. On A4 paper choose “Actual size”; on US Letter paper choose “Fit to page” so nothing is cut off.

The pack is suitable for ages 2–7. Each full book has its own age range: My First ABC Book is ages 2–4, My First Animal Explorer ages 3–6, My Little Community Helpers ages 4–7, and My Little Space Adventure ages 3–5.

Yes. Print as many copies as you need for your own household — a favorite page can be colored again and again.

The free pack is for personal and family use only. Please don't share the file, resell it or redistribute it — instead, tell friends to download their own free copy from this page.
