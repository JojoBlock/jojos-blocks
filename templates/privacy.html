Privacy Policy

This policy describes how this website handles personal information today. It will be updated if that changes.

JoJo’s Blocks is a brand website for digital PDF coloring books. This website itself takes no payment and does not use accounts or passwords. Purchase buttons on the homepage link to Gumroad, where checkout and payment happen — Gumroad's own privacy policy applies to anything you share with them at checkout.

Newsletter: if you enter your email address in the newsletter form on the homepage, we collect that address and store it in our subscriber list (a Google Sheet) so we can email you about new books and printable freebies. We never share or sell your email address. Emails are only sent when there is a new book or freebie to share — there is no automatic mailing schedule.

Analytics: we use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use this website — for example, which pages are visited, which books are viewed, and which Buy buttons are clicked. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects anonymous usage data, such as the pages visited, approximate location (country and city), device type, and how you arrived here (for example, from Pinterest or Google). It does not identify you personally, and we do not combine it with your email address. You can opt out using Google's analytics opt-out tools or by blocking cookies in your browser. We also use the Pinterest Tag, which records anonymous page visits so we can see whether visitors arrive from Pinterest. It does not identify you personally.

When you buy through Gumroad, Gumroad handles your payment details and delivery of the PDF — this website does not receive or store them.

Contact details for privacy questions: JoJo’s Blocks — jojobusiness0101@gmail.com.
