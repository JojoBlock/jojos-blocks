Refund & Support Policy

DRAFT — This policy is a working draft. It has not been approved by the owner of JoJo’s Blocks and has not received qualified legal review. It is not a final policy and must not be treated as one. The draft notice on this page will be removed only after owner approval and legal review.

JoJo’s Blocks sells digital PDF coloring books. You buy them on Gumroad, download the PDF, and print the pages at home. Because a digital file cannot be returned once it has been downloaded, this policy works differently from a refund policy for physical goods. It is written to be fair in both directions: genuine problems are always fixed, and a working download is not refunded simply because a buyer changed their mind.

If something is wrong with your file

If the file you received is corrupted, missing, incorrect, or unusable — for example, pages will not open, pages are blank, or you received the wrong book — contact us at any time. We will repair or replace the file. If the file cannot be fixed, we will refund your purchase. There is no time limit on reporting a defective file.

Accidental duplicate purchases

If you accidentally bought the same book twice, contact us and we will refund the duplicate purchase.

Change of mind

We do not offer refunds for a correctly delivered, working download simply because you changed your mind or did not like the book. Please read the book description, page count and preview information on the product page before buying. If you are unsure whether a book suits your child, email us first — we are happy to help you choose.

How to get help

Email jojobusiness0101@gmail.com. To help us fix the problem quickly, please include: the name of the book, the email address you used at checkout, and a short description of what went wrong (a screenshot helps if the file looks wrong). We aim to reply within 2 business days. This response time is a draft commitment for the owner to confirm.

Product improvements

When a book is corrected or improved, existing buyers receive the updated file free of charge. Contact us with your original order details and we will send the updated version.

Your legal rights

This policy does not take away any rights your local law gives you. In the European Union, consumers normally have a 14-day right to withdraw from an online purchase, but for digital content that right can be lost once downloading begins, if the buyer expressly agreed to immediate delivery and acknowledged losing the right — wording and consent that happen at checkout on Gumroad. Defective digital content gives the buyer repair, replacement or refund rights regardless of any store policy. In the United States there is no general federal cooling-off right for online purchases, but state consumer-protection laws, Gumroad’s platform terms and card-network chargebacks still apply. The legal wording of this policy needs qualified review before it is published as final.

Where purchases happen

Checkout, payment and delivery happen on Gumroad. Gumroad’s own terms also apply to your purchase, and Gumroad’s refund settings will be checked against this policy before it is published as final. This website takes no payment itself and does not store your payment details.
