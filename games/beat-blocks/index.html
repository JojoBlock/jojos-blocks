How to play 🎵

★ PERFECT! ★
▲
●
◆
watch the blocks land on the line
1

Pick a difficulty and one of the five original tunes, then press Play. Blocks slide down four coloured lanes.

2

Tap a lane — or press D F J K — exactly when its block crosses the glowing beat line. On a phone, tap the big coloured lane itself.

3

Perfect (right on the beat) scores 300, Good (a little early or late) scores 100, and a Miss scores nothing and breaks your combo. Each grade has its own colour, sound and pop!

4

Combos raise your multiplier up to ×3. Finish the song for your grade: S, A, B or C. Earn grade B or better to unlock harder difficulties — and new tunes!

5

Try Compose Mode: place blocks on the grid and hear your own tune loop. Bea dances when you’re on beat!
