Block Drop!

Blocks fall from the top. Move and rotate them to fill complete rows — full rows clear and score points.

  • 1 line = 100, 2 = 300, 3 = 500, 4 = 800 points (× level).
  • Every 10 lines the level rises and the blocks fall faster.
  • The pale outline shows where your block will land.

How to play

  • ← → or the on-screen arrows move the block; ↑ rotates it.
  • ↓ drops it one step; Space drops it all the way (hard drop).
  • P pauses. The game ends when a new block can't fit at the top.
  • Music is an optional light chiptune made in your browser — no audio file is downloaded.
