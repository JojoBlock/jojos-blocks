Click the Box!

A box pops up somewhere in the arena. Click it (or tap it) to score — then it jumps somewhere new.

  • Every hit scores 1 point. Fast hits in a row build your combo for bonus points.
  • The box shrinks a little with every hit — and in Blitz it starts small.
  • Space starts the game; P pauses it.

How to play

  • Click or tap the box each time it appears.
  • Combo: hit within 1.5 seconds of your last hit to raise your combo (×2, ×3…) — each hit then scores more.
  • Wait too long and the combo resets to ×1.
  • When the timer hits zero, your score is saved as your best if it beats it.
