📖 How to play

Watch the hand spin — tap the moment it touches the sun dot!

  1. 🕐 Tempo's hand spins around the glowing ring — faster after every hit.
  2. 🎯 Tap anywhere (or press Space) the moment the hand touches a glowing sun dot.
  3. ⭐ Perfect (dead centre) = 100 pts · Good (close) = 50 pts. Points grow with your speed level.
  4. ➕ Every 5 hits adds another target dot to the ring. At 10 hits a twin pair appears — two dots close together, hit either one!
  5. 🌀 From speed level 4, one dot starts drifting back and forth along the ring (teal halo) — time it as it moves.
  6. ⏱ Higher levels mean a smaller Perfect window — precision pays more as you get faster.
  7. 🌈 6 hits complete the rainbow ring — the bonus round reverses direction and pays double points!
  8. 💥 One miss ends the run. Beat your best score and streak!

Pause: P or Esc  •  Restart: R
