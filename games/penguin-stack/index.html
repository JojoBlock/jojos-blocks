Drop the swinging ice blocks, line them up perfectly, and build the tallest tower in the frozen bay. Watch out for the wind!

How to play 🐧

  1. 1Tap, click or press Space to drop the swinging ice block onto the tower.
  2. 2Any part that hangs over the edge gets sliced off — the tower gets narrower every miss-aimed drop!
  3. 3Land a perfect drop to keep the full width, grow your combo and earn bonus points. Pip hops with joy!
  4. 4From height 8, icy wind gusts push the swinging block — time your drop between gusts. Miss the tower completely and the run ends.
  5. 5Every 5 blocks the level rises and the swing gets faster. Every 10 blocks is a milestone worth +5 bonus points — and the wind gets stronger too!
