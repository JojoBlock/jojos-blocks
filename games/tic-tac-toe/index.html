Tic-Tac-Toe vs JoJo

Get three in a row — across, down or diagonally. You play X and go first.

  • Tap a square — or press keys 1–9 (top-left to bottom-right).
  • Easy: JoJo plays randomly. Normal: JoJo blocks and attacks. Hard: JoJo never loses.
  • Switch to 2 players to play a friend on the same screen.
  • Press P (or the Pause button) to pause; JoJo waits while you think.

How to play

  • Players take turns placing X and O on the 3×3 grid.
  • First to three in a row wins the round. A full board with no line is a draw.
  • Against JoJo, you are always X and move first; JoJo answers after a short think.
  • Keyboard: keys 1–9 place your mark, Tab + Enter works too.
  • Press P to pause the round, Escape to close this panel.
