How to play

Pip bounces on his own — you steer!

☀️ ☁️☁️ 🐧 ◀▶ Drag ◀ ▶ or use the arrow keys to steer
  • 👆Drag anywhere on the sky to steer Pip left and right. On a keyboard use ← → or A D.
  • ☁️Land on clouds to keep bouncing. The sky scrolls up as you climb — fall below the screen and the run ends.
  • ☀️Grab sun tokens. Every 5 suns charges a Sunny Burst — tap the Burst chip or press Space to rocket upwards. Grab suns quickly in a row for a sun streak!
  • 🌈Watch the clouds: 🟢 Moving — drifts sideways🟡 Spring — super bounce!🔴 Breakable — breaks after one bounce🟣 Crumbling — vanishes a few seconds after it appears
  • 🏆Every 25 blocks is a milestone — a banner, a fanfare and +1 Burst charge! Higher up, the clouds drift further apart and new cloud types appear.
  • 📱Tilt steering is optional (in Settings) and only works on phones with a tilt sensor — drag and arrow keys always work, on every device.
  • ⏸P or Esc pauses · R restarts · M mutes sound.
