Find the Hidden Animal!

Pick your animal and difficulty, then flip tiles to find where it is hiding. Every wrong flip uses up one attempt.

  • Tap a tile — or use Tab / arrow keys and press Enter.
  • Find the animal to score: more attempts left = more points.
  • Stuck? Use your one Hint per round to rule out a tile.

How to play

  • Choose an animal and a difficulty above.
  • Flip tiles to search. A ❌ means the animal is not there.
  • Find it before your attempts run out to win the round.
  • Score: 100 points per win + 25 per attempt left. Wins in a row build your streak.
  • Keyboard: Tab to move, Enter or Space to flip, arrow keys move between tiles.
