Flappy Penguin!

JoJo's penguin wants to fly. Help it flap through the pipes — every pipe cleared scores a point.

  • Tap, click or press Space to flap. Hold nothing — one flap per press.
  • Don't touch the pipes, the ground or the sky.
  • Medals: 🥉 10 pipes, 🥈 20 pipes, 🥇 30 pipes.

How to play

  • Flap to stay in the air; gravity pulls the penguin down.
  • Fly through each pipe gap to score. The pipes get a little faster as you score.
  • Pause with P or the Pause button. Restart any time.
  • Your best score is saved on this device.
