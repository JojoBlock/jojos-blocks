Memory Match

All the cards are face down. Flip two at a time and remember where each animal is hiding!

  • Tap a card — or use Tab / arrow keys and press Enter.
  • Moves count per pair of flips, not per card.
  • The timer starts on your first flip.

How to play

  • Flip any two cards. If they match, they stay face up.
  • If they don't match, they flip back — remember them!
  • Match every pair to win. Your best time and fewest moves are saved per difficulty.
  • Keyboard: Tab to move, Enter or Space to flip, arrow keys move between cards.
⏸

Paused

The timer is stopped and the cards are hidden. Take your time!
