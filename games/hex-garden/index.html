🌷 How to play

Watch: a piece drops in, the line blooms away! 🌸

  • Goal: bloom as many lines as you can and climb the levels — each level asks for more lines, and new piece shapes join the tray as you level up.
  • Plant: tap a piece in the tray, then tap a honeycomb cell — or drag the piece straight onto the board.
  • Rotate: tap 🔄 Rotate or press R to turn the selected piece 60°.
  • Bloom: fill a complete line in any of the three directions and it blooms away for points.
  • Bee bonus: every bloom calls Bella the bee — she drops a +50 bonus. Bloom again on your very next move and her bonus grows (+75, +100, up to +150) — keep the streak alive!
  • Scoring: 10 points per flower planted · 100 per line · extra lines in one move earn a combo bonus.
  • Levels: bloom the target number of lines (shown in the Level chip) to finish a level and earn up to ⭐⭐⭐. Level 3 brings Row of 4 & Blossom pieces, Level 4 Leaf, Level 5 Petal 5.
  • Game over: when none of your three pieces fits anywhere, the garden is full.
  • On your phone: tap a piece, then tap a cell — or drag it with your finger. Everything works with one thumb.
  • On a computer: 1–3 select a piece · Arrow keys move the garden cursor · Enter/Space plant · R rotate · P pause.
