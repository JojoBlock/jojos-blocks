About JoJo's Blocks

A small store with a growing shelf

JoJo's Blocks began with children's colouring books — a first experiment in making things worth printing. Today it is a small, professional digital store: our own books and downloads, free resources, browser games, and honest recommendations, all made to help families and creative people learn, play and make things.

Every book is a digital PDF: buy it, download it instantly, print the pages at home and colour them again and again. No printed books are shipped. Support replies within 2 business days on weekdays.
