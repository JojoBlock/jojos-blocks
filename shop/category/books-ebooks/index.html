Questions about the books

What are coloring books?

Coloring books are collections of black-and-white line illustrations for children (and grown-ups) to color in with crayons, pencils or markers. Ours are digital PDF coloring books you can print at home.

Which age groups are the books suitable for?

Each book shows its recommended age range on its product card and on its book page. My First ABC Book is ages 2–4, My First Animal Explorer ages 3–6, My Little Community Helpers ages 4–7, and My Little Space Adventure ages 3–5.
