Why Parents Love Our Books

Colouring is play — and play is how children learn

Colouring gives children a calm, creative activity they can enjoy on their own or together with you. These are general benefits of creative play, not medical claims about any individual child.

🎨

Creativity & imagination

Blank pages invite children to choose colours, invent stories and make each picture their own.

✏️

Fine motor practice

Holding crayons and colouring inside shapes gives little hands everyday practice with grip and control.

🧩

Learning through play

Themed pages can introduce animals, letters, numbers and the world around us in a relaxed, playful way.

📵

Screen-free time

A book, some crayons and a quiet table — a simple alternative to screens for car rides, waiting rooms and rainy days.

👨‍👩‍👧

Time together

Colouring side by side is an easy way to talk, laugh and share a calm moment as a family.

🖨️

Print again & again

With a printable PDF, a favourite page can be printed fresh whenever a child wants to colour it differently.

About Our Brand

A creative destination for young colourists

JoJo’s Blocks is a children's coloring book brand. We make coloring books that children enjoy and parents can trust for simple, screen-free fun.

Our books are digital PDF coloring books. Buy the PDF, print the pages at home, and colour them again and again.

  • Original, child-friendly illustrations — no copied characters
  • Clear, age-appropriate themes (details added per book)
  • Digital PDF format, ready to print at home
  • Designed for home, school and travel

Each book is made to be simple to use: clear illustrations and pages that print well at home.
