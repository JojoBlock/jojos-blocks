Ho, ho, ho — Christmas fun starts here!

Join Santa Claus and his best friend Rudy the Reindeer for 56 pages of Christmas coloring, games, and learning fun, made just for kids ages 4–8!

Every page is a new adventure. Color Santa, snowmen, gingerbread houses, penguins, and a friendly ghost in a Santa hat. Then play your way through mazes, I Spy, spot the difference, matching, dot-to-dots, and color by number — with Rudy cheering you on with a tip on every page.

34 Christmas colouring pages, from Santa to the snow globe

Easy pages (pp.2–18) start with big, simple scenes — Santa, ornaments, gingerbread man, snowman, tree, Rudolph, penguin, stockings, candy canes, wreath, hot cocoa, puppy, elf, the Nativity, bells, angel and a friendly ghost. Medium pages (pp.19–30) add detail — gingerbread house, sleigh, workshop, snow globe, family decorating the tree, nutcracker, snowman family, polar bear, chimney, cookies, kitten and presents. Tricky pages (pp.31–35) are the most detailed — the reindeer team, a steam train, a winter village, a blanket fort and the North Pole toy factory. One tiny hidden candy cane hides on every single colouring page.

12 games & puzzles — mazes, I Spy and spot the difference

Three mazes (help Santa reach the tree, Rudolph find the sleigh, and escape the North Pole Workshop), two spot-the-difference puzzles, two I Spy seek-and-count pages, animal-to-gift matching, connect-the-dots to 25, colour by number, draw the other half, and a Letter to Santa page with a Christmas wish list. Rudy the Reindeer gives a tip on every games page.

6 learning pages — counting, tracing, phonics & patterns

Counting & ten-frames 1–10, trace & write (SANTA, TREE, STAR, GIFT, SNOW, ELF), Christmas alphabet & phonics match, shapes & pattern sequences, a mini scavenger hunt & holiday bingo, and 6 amazing Christmas fun facts.

Frequently asked questions

What ages is the Christmas Coloring & Activity Book for?
Ages 4-8. Every colouring and games page is labelled Easy, Medium or Tricky, so younger children start with big, simple pages and older children get detailed scenes and harder puzzles.

How many pages are in the Christmas book, and what is inside?
56 pages: 34 Christmas colouring pages, 12 games and puzzles, 6 learning pages, full answer keys and an Official North Pole Christmas Explorer Certificate. Rudy the reindeer guides children through the games, and there is a hidden candy cane to find on every colouring page.

What games and puzzles does it include?
Three mazes, two spot-the-difference puzzles, two I Spy seek-and-count pages, a matching game, connect-the-dots to 25, colour by number, draw the other half, and a Letter to Santa page with a Christmas wish list.

Does the book have answer keys?
Yes. Pages 54-55 give the solutions for all three mazes, both spot-the-difference puzzles, both I Spy pages, matching, connect-the-dots, colour by number, counting, phonics, patterns and draw the other half.
