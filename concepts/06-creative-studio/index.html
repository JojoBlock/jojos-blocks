● The Studio

A small studio,
making useful
beautiful things.

JoJo’s Blocks started with one experiment: children’s colouring books. The books were the beginning, not the whole story. Today the studio makes books, games, printables and tools — and sells them directly, honestly, and without fuss.

Everything here is designed in-house: the books, the games, the free resources and the writing. What you see is what you get — digital PDFs you print at home, games you play in the browser, and free downloads with no sign-up and no catch.
